Red Lentil Soup with Chicken and Turnips

◷  55 min ♜  8 servings 🔥  1866 cal
High Protein Dairy-Free Very Healthy
Overview Ingredients Nutrition Instructions

A hearty and nutritious soup made with red lentils, chicken, and fresh vegetables. Perfect for a healthy and comforting meal.

Ingredients
Red Lentils
Chicken Breast
Turnips
Carrots
Celery
Nutrition Facts
(Per Serving)
Calories   1866
Protein   11 g
Carbs    59 g
Fat     6 g
Instructions
1. Add the red lentils, chicken breast, and vegetables into a large pot.
2. Add vegetable broth and bring to a boil.
3. Reduce heat and simmer until lentils are tender and chicken is cooked.
4. Remove chicken, shred it, and return to the pot.
5. Season with salt, pepper, and herbs to taste.
6. Serve warm and enjoy your nutritious homemade soup.
