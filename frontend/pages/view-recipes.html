Grilled Chicken Bowl

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast
  • Cooked rice
  • Bell peppers
  • Olive oil
  • Seasoning

Instructions

Season and cook the chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F. Cook the rice and vegetables, then combine everything in a bowl.

Miso Glazed Tofu Bowl

Ingredients

  • Firm tofu
  • Miso paste
  • Cooked rice
  • Broccoli
  • Carrots

Instructions

Coat the tofu with miso glaze and bake until heated through. Serve with cooked rice and vegetables.

Greek Salad Bowl

Ingredients

  • Lettuce
  • Cucumber
  • Tomatoes
  • Feta cheese
  • Olives
  • Olive oil

Instructions

Wash and chop the vegetables. Combine them with feta cheese and olives, then toss with olive oil.
