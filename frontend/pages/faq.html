Accounts & Profile

Do I need an account to use Better Bite Orlando?

No. You can browse publicly available recipes, restaurants, grocery stores, farmers' markets, food pantries, and educational resources without creating an account.

An account is required to access account-specific settings and available personalization features.

You must be at least 18 years of age to register for an account.

Create an Account
How do I reset my password?

If you forget your password, follow these steps:

  1. Open the sign-in page.
  2. Select Forgot Password.
  3. Enter the email address associated with your account.
  4. Check your email for a password reset link.
  5. Follow the link to create a new password.

Password reset links expire after 15 minutes for security purposes. If your link expires, submit another password reset request.

If you do not receive the email, check your spam or junk mail folder.

Reset Your Password
Can I update my profile information?

Registered users can sign in to access their profile and manage available account information.

Sign in to your account and navigate to your profile to view the available account settings.

Sign In to Your Account
How do I delete my account?

You may request deletion of your Better Bite Orlando account and associated personal information through our Contact Us form.

  1. Open the Contact Us page.
  2. Enter your name and the email address associated with your account.
  3. Select Delete My Data as the subject.
  4. Explain that you are requesting account deletion.
  5. Submit your request.

We may need to verify your identity before processing the request. Account deletion is not automatic upon submission.

Never include your password in your message.

For additional information, review the Data Retention section of our Privacy Policy.

Request Account Deletion

Finding Food & Recipes

How do I find a grocery store, farmers' market, or food pantry near me?

Use the Groceries page to discover local food resources.

  1. Open the Groceries page.
  2. Enter your ZIP code.
  3. Search for nearby food locations.
  4. Use the available category filters to refine your results.
  5. View locations on the interactive map or in the results list.

Where available, location details and directions are provided to help you plan your visit.

How are food recommendations determined?

Food discovery results are based on the search criteria and filters you select, together with information available from our third-party data providers.

Depending on the page, results may be influenced by:

  • Your selected ZIP code or location.
  • Your search terms.
  • Selected dietary filters.
  • Selected food location categories.
  • Available recipe and restaurant information.

Results are intended to help you explore food options and make informed choices. They are not individualized medical or nutritional recommendations.

Can I save grocery locations, restaurants, and recipes?

The Favorites feature is intended to help registered users save food locations, restaurants, and recipes for future reference.

This functionality is currently under development and may not be available for all resource types.

You can continue to browse and search for food resources without using Favorites.

Using Better Bite Orlando

Can I access Better Bite Orlando on my phone?

Yes. The Better Bite Orlando website is designed to support desktop computers, tablets, and smartphones.

You can access the website through a compatible web browser on your device. No app installation is required to use the website.

View the User Guide
Where does the information come from?

Better Bite Orlando uses external data providers to help users discover local food resources, restaurants, recipes, and nutritional information.

  • Geoapify: Location searches and available business information.
  • Spoonacular: Recipe, ingredient, dietary, and nutritional information.
  • OpenStreetMap: Map imagery used to display nearby food locations.
  • Google Maps: External directions links.

Third-party information may change over time. We recommend confirming important details, such as operating hours and food availability, directly with the applicable provider.

Additional information is available in our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.
