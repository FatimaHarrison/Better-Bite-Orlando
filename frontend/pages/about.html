About Better Bite Orlando

Driving in turning Everyday Meals into Better Opportunities for Health.

Our Mission

Building healthier communities throughout Orlando.

In today's society healthy eating can be found predominantly challenging for individuals to start a clean diet. Roughly about thirty one percent of the Central Florida population live in obesity. Travel can also be difficult if you don’t have a car, live far from a grocery store, or just don’t know what’s nearby. Better Bite Orlando is implemented to help individuals start a new journey with better health. We want to ensure that users have easy access and provide transportation to find fresh food and nutritious options in healthy restaurants, grocery stores, and food pantries close to you. Our recipe page will save safe instructions and search features to find healthy meals that are realistic for everyday life. We believe wellness starts with what you eat in being proud to serve our community by helping them find food that supports health, energy, and balance.

Our Purpose

Removing barriers to healthy eating.

At some point in life you may have that thought in trying something new. A healthy habit you may want to keep or a hobby to adopt a change towards a new diet. We create a system for communities in Central Florida to divert health by giving residents access to resources to help them reach that goal. In our knowledge many individuals may face barriers such as limited transportation, assistance with navigating to grocery stores and restaurants, and financial challenges, and uncertainty about where to begin their wellness journey. Our platform bridges those gaps by connecting residents with nutritious restaurants, grocery stores, food pantries, transportation resources, and practical, easy guided recipes that support healthier daily choices. By providing trusted information and local resources in one convenient place, we empower Central Florida residents to make informed decisions, develop sustainable eating habits, and take meaningful steps toward better health. Wellness starts with what you eat, and we are committed to helping our community build healthier to bring a stronger future one bite at a time.

Our Story

Created by Valencia College students.

Better Bite Orlando began as a Software Development Project at Valencia College. What started as a classroom project evolved into a platform designed to help Orlando residents find healthier food options, discover local resources, and improve their quality of life through better nutrition.

Helping people in Orlando find healthier food without the hassle. We wanted one place where user can easily find restaurants, grocery stores, and healthy options in Florida. Were still growing and wanting to expand our journey across the world. and improving, and we'd love to hear from you.

Healthy People. Stronger Communities.

Better Bite Orlando is committed to helping Central Florida residents build healthier lifestyles one bite at a time.
