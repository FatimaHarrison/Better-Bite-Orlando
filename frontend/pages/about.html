Better Bite Orlando

Turning everyday meals into better opportunities for health.

Our Mission

Building healthier communities throughout Orlando.

Our mission is to make healthy eating more accessible to Central Florida residents. Finding nutritious food can be challenging, especially for individuals facing limited transportation, financial constraints, or uncertainty about available resources.

Better Bite Orlando helps residents discover healthy restaurants, grocery stores, farmers' markets, and food pantries in their community. Our platform also provides nutritious recipes and educational resources that support informed food choices and healthier everyday habits.

Our Purpose

Removing barriers to healthy eating.

We believe everyone should have access to nutritious food and the information needed to make healthier choices. However, finding affordable and accessible food options is not always easy.

Better Bite Orlando brings local food resources, healthy recipes, nutrition education, and transportation information together in one convenient platform. By simplifying access to these resources, we hope to empower Central Florida residents to develop sustainable eating habits and take meaningful steps toward better health.

Our Story

Created by Valencia College students.

Better Bite Orlando began as a Software Development Project at Valencia College. What started as a classroom project evolved into a platform designed to help Orlando residents find healthier food options, discover local resources, and make informed nutritional decisions.

Our team combined its knowledge of software development, research, and user experience design to create an accessible and practical resource for the community. We welcome feedback as we continue to improve the platform.

Healthy People. Stronger Communities.

Better Bite Orlando is committed to helping Central Florida residents build healthier lifestyles, one bite at a time.
