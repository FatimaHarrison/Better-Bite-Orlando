Grilled Rosemary Chicken Bowl

Tender grilled chicken with fresh rosemary, quinoa, roasted vegetables, and a light lemon dressing. A simple, nutritious and flavorful meal.

Prep Time 15 mins
Cook Time 20 mins
Total Time 35 mins
Servings 4
High Protein Gluten Free Healthy Lunch/Dinner

Ingredients

  • 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp fresh rosemary (chopped)
  • 1 tsp garlic (minced)
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 red bell pepper (sliced)
  • 1 cup quinoa (uncooked)
  • 2 cups water or low-sodium broth
  • 1 zucchini (sliced)
  • 2 Cups mixed greens
  • 1/4 cup feta cheese (crumbled)
  • 1/4 cup lemon juice
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Why It's a Better Bite

  • High in protein
  • Packed with vegetables
  • Fresh ingredients
  • Great for lunch or dinner

Instructions

1

Marinate the chicken

In a bowl, combine olive oil, garlic, rosemary, salt and pepper. Coat the chicken and let it marinate for 15 minutes (or up to 2 hours).

2

Cook the chicken

Grill or pan-sear over medium heat for 6 to 7 minutes per side, until fully cooked. Let it rest, then slice.

3

Roast the vegetables

Toss zucchini and bell pepper with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast at 400F for 15-20 minutes, until tender.

4

Assemble the bowl

Add quinoa, mixed greens, chicken, roasted vegetables, and feta. Drizzle with lemon and season to taste.

Quick Tip

Use rotisserie chicken or precooked quinoa to save time.

Nutrition Facts

520 Calories
42g Protein
48g Carbs
18g Fat
