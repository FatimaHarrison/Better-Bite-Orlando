1. Do I Need an Account to Use Better Bite Orlando?

Some features may be available without signing in. However, creating an account is required to save favorites, personalize recommendations, and access account-specific settings and preferences.

2. How Do I Reset My Password?

Select Forgot Password on the login page and follow the password reset instructions sent to your email address. Password reset links expire after 15 minutes for security purposes.

10. Where do you get the information from?

We obtain information from trusted third-party APIs and data providers. Place details such as names, locations, ratings, hours, and other business information are retrieved through external location and business APIs. Recipe content, including ingredients and nutritional information, is sourced through third-party recipe and nutrition APIs. We do not create this information ourselves; instead, we display data provided by these external services to help users discover restaurants and recipes.
