1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you register for a Better Bite Orlando account, we collect the following information:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • ZIP code
  • Password

This information is used to create and manage your account and support available personalization features. Passwords are protected using secure, one-way password hashing and are not stored in plain text.

Contact Information

When you submit a message through our Contact Us form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The selected subject
  • The contents of your message

This information is used to respond to your inquiry, provide support, or process privacy-related requests.

Search Information

When you search for grocery stores, farmers' markets, food pantries, restaurants, or recipes, we process information such as ZIP codes, search terms, dietary filters, and other selected search criteria.

You do not need to create an account to access publicly available food and nutrition resources.

2. Account Preferences

Better Bite Orlando may collect and store preferences that you choose to provide through available account features.

These may include:

  • Dietary preferences
  • Preferred ZIP code
  • Favorite grocery stores and food locations
  • Favorite restaurants
  • Saved recipes

Where supported, this information is used to personalize your experience and make it easier to access your saved information.

3. Data Usage

When you access our website, our servers and service providers may process technical information associated with your requests.

This information may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Dates and times of access
  • Requested pages and API resources
  • Technical errors and request information

This information helps us operate the website, troubleshoot technical issues, and maintain the security and reliability of our services.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and support account recovery.
  • Display location-based food resources.
  • Provide relevant search results and recommendations.
  • Support available favorites and preference features.
  • Respond to inquiries and support requests.
  • Send account recovery and contact-related emails.
  • Maintain website security and functionality.
  • Investigate and resolve technical issues.
  • Respond to applicable legal obligations.

5. Food, Restaurant, and Recipe Data

Better Bite Orlando uses third-party data providers to help users discover local food resources, restaurants, and recipes.

Geoapify

Geoapify provides geocoding and location-based information. ZIP codes, geographic coordinates, and relevant search criteria may be transmitted to Geoapify to retrieve food locations and restaurant information.

Spoonacular

Spoonacular provides recipe, ingredient, and nutritional information. Recipe search terms, dietary filters, and recipe identifiers may be transmitted to retrieve relevant content.

OpenStreetMap

Our interactive maps use OpenStreetMap map tiles. When a map is displayed, your browser may communicate directly with the map tile service and provide technical information associated with those requests.

Google Maps

We provide external links to Google Maps for directions and transportation information. If you follow these links, your interaction with Google Maps is governed by Google's own privacy practices.

Information obtained from third-party providers remains subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. Authentication and Security

We use reasonable technical safeguards designed to protect your account information and reduce the risk of unauthorized access.

These safeguards include:

  • Secure, one-way password hashing.
  • Protected authentication cookies.
  • Encrypted HTTPS connections to our hosted services.
  • Password reset verification procedures.
  • Time-limited password reset tokens.
  • Access controls and request rate limiting.

Although we take reasonable precautions, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Better Bite Orlando uses authentication cookies to maintain signed-in sessions and support access to account-related functionality.

These cookies are designed to support secure authentication and help protect user accounts.

Third-party resources used by our website may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy practices.

You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may prevent you from signing in or using certain account features.

8. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate our website and provide its functionality.

Hosting and Infrastructure

Our hosted application and database services use DigitalOcean infrastructure to support website operation and data storage.

Email Delivery

We use SMTP2GO to support email delivery. Information necessary to deliver messages, including email addresses and message contents, may be processed by this service.

External Website Resources

Our website uses external resources such as Google Fonts and content delivery networks, including jsDelivr and unpkg.

Your browser may provide technical information, such as your IP address, to these providers when downloading fonts, scripts, stylesheets, or other resources.

Other Necessary Disclosures

Information may also be disclosed:

  • When required by applicable law.
  • To protect the security of our services.
  • To investigate misuse or unauthorized access.
  • To protect the rights and safety of users.

9. Data Retention

Our approach is to retain personal information only as reasonably necessary to operate our services, maintain accounts, address support requests, maintain security, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Contact Form Messages

Contact form submissions are transmitted through our email delivery service and are not stored in our application database.

Messages may be retained in email inboxes or by email service providers as necessary to respond to inquiries and maintain correspondence.

Requesting Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by following these steps:

  1. Open our Contact Us page.
  2. Enter the email address associated with your account.
  3. Select Delete My Data from the subject dropdown.
  4. Explain that you would like to request account and personal information deletion.
  5. Submit your request.

We may need to verify your identity before processing a deletion request. Submitting the form does not automatically delete your account.

We will make reasonable efforts to remove or anonymize information that is no longer necessary. Certain information may need to be retained for legal, security, backup, dispute resolution, or operational purposes.

Please do not include your password or other sensitive account credentials in your request.

10. User Rights

Depending on applicable laws and the nature of your request, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Manage available account preferences.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal information.
  • Exercise other applicable privacy rights.

Available account settings may be managed through your profile. Other privacy-related requests may be submitted through our Contact Us page.

11. Children's Privacy

Better Bite Orlando allows users to browse publicly available educational and food-related resources without creating an account.

Users under the age of 18 should access these resources under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian.

Account registration is restricted to individuals who are at least 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from individuals under 18 through account registration.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a minor in violation of this policy, we will take reasonable steps to remove that information.

Parents or legal guardians who believe their child's personal information has been collected may contact us through our Contact Us page.

12. Third-Party Links

Our website may include links to external websites, recipes, restaurants, grocery stores, transportation resources, and other organizations.

We do not control the privacy practices or content of these external websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

13. User Account Responsibilities

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials and taking reasonable steps to protect their accounts.

We encourage users to:

  • Use a strong, unique password.
  • Avoid sharing account credentials.
  • Sign out when using shared devices.
  • Notify us if unauthorized account access is suspected.

If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact Better Bite Orlando promptly.

14. Nutrition & Medical Disclaimer

Better Bite Orlando provides educational information about nutrition, healthy eating, recipes, food resources, and wellness.

Information provided through our website is for educational and informational purposes only and is not intended to replace professional medical or healthcare advice.

Our content is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or health condition.

Users should consult a qualified healthcare professional before making significant dietary, nutritional, or health-related decisions.

Additional information is available in our Terms and Conditions.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes to our services, data practices, or applicable requirements.

Changes will be posted on this page together with an updated revision date. We encourage users to review this policy periodically.
